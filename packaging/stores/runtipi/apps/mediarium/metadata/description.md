# Mediarium

Mediarium is an all-in-one media manager for movies, TV, music, ebooks and audiobooks. Find a title and it searches your Usenet and torrent indexers, downloads the best release with its built-in Usenet downloader and torrent client, unpacks it, names it the way you chose and puts it in your library, ready for Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, Silo, Audiobookshelf or Kavita.

It does the jobs of Radarr, Sonarr, Prowlarr, SABnzbd, a torrent client and Bazarr in one app, with a built-in list of indexer sites (no Prowlarr needed), quality profiles, automatic upgrades, subtitles, a request portal and family accounts, notifications, a built-in VPN for torrents and optional single sign-on.

## Getting started

1. Open Mediarium and follow the setup wizard to create your administrator account.
2. Your library lives in Runtipi's media folder: movies in `movies`, TV in `tv`, Mediarium's downloads in `downloads/mediarium`. For music, ebooks or audiobooks, set the optional music, ebooks and audiobooks folders in the container settings.
3. Add an indexer and, for Usenet, your provider. You can change everything else later under Settings.

Mediarium does not provide or host any content. You are responsible for the indexers you add and what you download.
